Frequently asked questions

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes, bring your card. We provide receipts for reimbursement. Ask via the contact form for your provider.

What should I expect at my first visit?

A short consultation, a gentle exam, and time to ask questions before any treatment begins. Ideal for anxious patients and kids.

At what age can children start visiting?

From age 3, or when the first teeth appear. First visits are short, friendly, and parent accompanied.

How do I reschedule an appointment?

Use the Contact → Send enquiry form or call +94 11 047 0471 at least 24h ahead. Online re-booking takes under 2 minutes.

Do whitening / veneers hurt?

No, we use gentle, modern techniques and explain every step. Sensitivity, if any, is temporary.
